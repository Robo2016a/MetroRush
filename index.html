ABOUT METRO RUSH
An endless runner built entirely in the browser — no downloads, no installs

🎮 How to Play

  • Run down 3 lanes of a night-time metro track
  • Dodge barriers, poles, cones, and low obstacles
  • Collect gold coins — they power your shop
  • Speed increases every few seconds
  • Survive as long as possible for a high score

⌨️ Controls

Keyboard:

←→ or AD — Move lanes

↑ W Space — Jump

↓ S — Slide under obstacles

Mobile: Swipe in any direction

⚡ Power-Ups

  • 🧲 Magnet — Sucks in nearby coins (10s)
  • 🛡️ Shield — Absorbs one fatal hit
  • ⚡ 2x Score — Doubles all scoring (15s)
  • 🚀 Jetpack — Fly over everything (8s)
  • ⏱️ Slow Time — Halves obstacle speed (12s)

💡 Tips

  • Check the shop every day for your free daily coins
  • Save Jetpack for long straight obstacle runs
  • Stack 2x Score with Magnet for maximum coin value
  • Shield is best saved as a "get out of jail" safety net
  • Slow Time is great when speed gets out of control
